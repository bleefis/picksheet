# FS Weekly Order: Quick Reference

**Print this and keep it at the store.**

App: [picksheet.netlify.app](https://picksheet.netlify.app) (home-screen icon **FS Order**)

---

## Every week

1. Open **FS Order**
2. Walk the store, tap circles to set cases
3. **Review & send** → **Send order**
4. Wait for the **Carts loaded** email
5. Tap **Open Four Seasons to submit**
6. Submit **Produce**, then switch to **Meat & Dairy** and submit

The robot never submits. You always do the last step.

---

## Tapping

| Do this | Result |
|---|---|
| Tap a circle | Add a case (after 4 it goes back to empty) |
| Hold a circle 1 sec | Clear it |
| Tap a product with ▼ | Show its varieties, cheapest first |
| **Reload it** (empty screen) | Refill last week's order |
| **See how it went** | Reopen the cart progress after sending |

---

## Prices

- **Bold** = price per piece or per lb. Small = case price.
- **CHEAPEST** and **ON AD** tags show the best deals.
- Prices refresh every morning from 6:30 AM.

---

## Emails you'll get

| Email | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Four Seasons order · *date* | Your order, with CSV files |
| Carts loaded | Ready to review and submit |
| Cart problem | Four Seasons was down. Ask for a rerun or import the CSVs by hand |
| Prices didn't refresh | Open Setup and refresh |
| Four Seasons Price Check (Monday) | Prices that changed. Update the register |

---

## If something's off

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Old version on phone | Close the app fully, reopen |
| "Already logged in" on Four Seasons | Robot is working. Wait a minute |
| Product missing | Setup → check **On phone** is on |
